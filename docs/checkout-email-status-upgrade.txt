Implementation
- Read existing persisted Resend open/click facts per send in the bounded checkout query.
- Display one green status: Recovered > Clicked > Opened > Sent.
- Recovery uses verified checkout evidence and the latest applicable accepted send in the selected journey; it does not write email attribution or revenue.
- No recovery status on an unsent email. Delivery does not imply an open.
- Existing Waiting/countdown/Not applicable and other unsent states preserved.
- Existing 12-second read cache and 15-second component refresh retained.
- Removed recipient timeline expanders and their mount calls. Backend timeline, schedule and processing functions retained.
- No schema migration, scheduling, sending, content, flow activation, production writes, commit, push or deployment.

Tracking limitations
- Opened means a recorded provider open event. Privacy prefetching/scanners can produce opens without a human reading; blocked images can hide actual opens. Resend's documented opened payload has no human-versus-proxy discriminator, so this change does not invent one or delete historical events.
  https://resend.com/docs/webhooks/emails/opened
  https://resend.com/docs/knowledge-base/why-are-my-open-rates-not-accurate
- Clicked means a recorded Resend tracked-link event; the existing ledger does not prove that the actor was human rather than a link scanner.
- Existing tracking must be enabled at the provider and signed webhooks must arrive and reconcile to the stored provider receipt. Production configuration was not modified or exercised with real sends.
- Historical unrecorded opens/clicks cannot be reconstructed. Existing persisted events are reused on reload.
- Recovery requires exact shop-scoped checkout-token/order evidence, signed completion, or Admin completedAt data. Customer email alone is not recovery evidence. Unmatched orders remain unconfirmed until existing reconciliation supplies evidence.
- Recovered on the latest applicable sent email is a checkout outcome display convention, not causal attribution to that email.
