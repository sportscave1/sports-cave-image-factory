Diagnosis and confidence
Receipt delivery, signature verification, durable acceptance and eventual
registration are confirmed. The delay was downstream of receipt acceptance.
Production already queues receipts; the original dirty checkout had older
synchronous receiver code and was NOT used as the repair baseline.
The worker had an unsupervised daemon thread sharing its process with other
background work. Batches and scans did not check shutdown, despite a ten-minute
renewable ownership lease and bounded supervisor shutdown. Queue ordering favored
newest receipts. These are verified recovery/scheduling defects.
Historical logs do not prove whether this particular stall was a blocked call,
an abandoned lease, or another thread failure. Do not claim a proven exception,
network outage, or Shopify delivery failure. No error for this product was found.

Repair
- Existing supervisor launches product onboarding as a child process, with the
  existing five-second restart policy and independent connection/module state.
- Child handles SIGTERM/SIGINT. Drain and scan stop between work items; tick's
  finally block releases ownership. Interrupted scan pages remain replayable.
- Operational ticks handle five receipts and at most ten scan candidates;
  continuation pages remain due after five seconds, interleaved with deliveries.
- Fresh delivery lane is oldest-first; reserved retry capacity is retained.
- Lease contention and completed tick counts now appear in logs. Failed/blocked
  scans are not incorrectly postponed for five minutes.
- Script entry uses the canonical module to preserve PendingRegistration type
  identity across backend imports.

The app's managed-worktree mechanism provided task isolation at the deployed SHA;
no other task checkout or original dirty working files were modified.
The user explicitly authorized commit, push and deployment, followed by live
verification. The task branch was fast-forwarded to current origin/main
79c5d896044b88df8b48373e7461a17972447001; none of the four changed code/test files

Release uses the existing main-branch auto-deploy configuration. No schema change,
service creation, Blueprint sync or product replay is required. The deployment
must be verified live with child startup and receipt progress before completion.
This is reliability hardening, not an absolute never-fails guarantee. A hard kill

remain important. Additional process memory has not been measured on Render.
