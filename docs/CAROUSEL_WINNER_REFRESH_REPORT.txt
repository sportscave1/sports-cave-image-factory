Each card gets independent observed role, scene family, useful visual/copy principle, keep/change/upgrade, and a materially new environment. Bedroom -> new bedroom, man cave -> new man cave, office -> new office, lounge -> new lounge, detail role -> new detail execution. Concrete architecture/layout/wall/furniture/camera/lighting declarations reject generic claims or recolour/angle-only plans. Final pixel fidelity and aesthetic quality still require human inspection.

Source primary texts remain separate. Each card retains its own image/headline/description/order. New shared primary variations and card copy must substantively refresh the source; textual duplicate/reorder checks and verified fixed-fact exceptions apply. No invented performance or scarcity claims.
