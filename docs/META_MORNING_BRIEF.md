# Meta CRM Morning Brief

Sports Updates and Meta CRM analysis are scheduled daily at 06:00 Australia/Sydney in ChatGPT. Use the existing subscription and signed-in tools. The Sports publisher checks for the completed Sydney-date edition at 06:45, with a fallback check at 08:45. It keeps the last published brief if today's is unavailable. The Meta publisher can publish as soon as its own review completes, even if today's sports edition is still pending.

## Daily ChatGPT prompt

> Review Sports Cave's entire Meta ad account with the connected read-only reporting and Sports Cave OS tools. Review every accessible active campaign, ad set and ad, including its delivery status, spend, impressions, reach, frequency, clicks, link clicks, CTR, CPC, CPM, engagement, likes, adds to cart, checkout starts, purchases, purchase value and ROAS. Compare the last closed reporting day with the prior day and compare the last seven closed days with the preceding seven. Use the account's reporting timezone, currency and attribution window and identify them alongside the date range. Use the connector's freshest saved account totals when ad-level data is unavailable. Mark missing or stale data explicitly and distinguish a confirmed zero from unavailable data. Do not infer which ads are active or winning from aggregate totals. Keep Shopify sales and Meta-attributed purchases distinct.
>
> Review historical creatives and paused ads across the available history for candidates to refresh or retest. Compare like-for-like objectives and attribution settings. Use Sports Cave OS and Shopify to confirm product availability and recent demand when those sources are accessible. Read today's Sports Updates Morning Brief when available and consider verified upcoming events, dates and active products. If today's sports brief is still pending, use the latest published edition and state its date. Explain which ads to keep running or watch, which creative to refresh or relaunch, and which product or creative to test. Prioritise up to five actions by evidence and urgency. Give each action a specific ad or product where verified, a reason, and what to check next. Keep proposals conditional when data is incomplete. Do not make any ad, budget or store changes.
>
> Produce a short plain-text summary and the daily metric snapshot for OS Home. Return a JSON object with date (today in Sydney, YYYY-MM-DD), coverage (up to 600 characters), summary (up to 900 characters), metrics (use spend, impressions, reach, frequency, clicks, link_clicks, ctr, cpc, cpm, engagement, likes, add_to_cart, checkouts, purchases, purchase_value and roas as keys with formatted string values or null), and recommendations (up to five plain-text strings of up to 420 characters each). Include the reporting dates and any access gaps in coverage. Exclude customer details and credentials. If all reporting is inaccessible, preserve the previous edition.

After the OS change is deployed, use the signed-in OS reporting owner session to submit the completed edition at `/ops/meta-morning-brief/publish`. Check the Home panel first and skip publication if today's Meta edition is already visible. Confirm the success message and the Sydney date on Home. The private handoff stores the latest edition in existing OS settings and uses the same owner-session publishing mechanism as Sports Updates.
