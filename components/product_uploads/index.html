Product Uploads

How to
  1. Choose Draft, Live or Update Existing.
  2. Choose the PSD from Dropbox Review, or browse Designs Live for an existing product.
  3. Wait for Artwork ready (or retry preparation if it fails), then Generate mockups.
  4. Use Copy black frame, paste the image into ChatGPT, then Copy prompt and paste the master brief. Approve each separate room image before generating the next; upload each finished image to its matching Man Cave, Office or Living Room card.
  5. Click any preview to open its full-resolution original. Copy black frame retrieves original pixels only when clicked. For other images, open the original before using browser Copy image; copying a lightweight preview copies that preview.
  6. Save all opens Dropbox at product-images. Choose the sport collection and Save here; the current Product name creates the output folder automatically. If a transfer fails, retry the same destination to check and skip confirmed files.
  7. Open the saved design folder, attach its images to ChatGPT, and paste the selected Shopify prompt.
  8. Let ChatGPT finish the Shopify upload or update.
  9. Return here, paste the exact Shopify product name and press Upload.
  10. The app links the exact PSD to the confirmed Shopify product. Companion renames it to the product title. Drafts stay in Review; completed published products move to Designs Live. Retry file handoff if the source changed or a filename conflicts.

The separate ChatGPT conversation is controlled by you. The manual workflow remains available, including prompt-only uploads and Edition Ops mapping, reset and retry.
