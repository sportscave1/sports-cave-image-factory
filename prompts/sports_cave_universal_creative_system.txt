Any extra text, missing field or wrapped field requires correction in that existing review before delivery. Correct only the footer while retaining the approved room and complete framed edition. Inspect the exported JPEG again at full size and mobile size. Emit the completion marker only after that exported image has exactly these three lines; report an unresolved defect honestly instead of claiming verification.

Applies only to a three-cover Instant Experience task, never Carousel or Single Image.

Choose a private collector study or a sunlit reading lounge, whichever is distinct from the other completed rooms. A downloaded/display error requires recovery of the existing bytes, not another image generation.
