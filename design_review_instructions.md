**Shared collector Edit workflow**

Edit uses the New Design prompt renderer, including its research, composition, single generation, collector styling and Review checks. Do not maintain separate creative defaults or generation counts here.

The Edit text-box request takes creative priority, and the selected artwork supplies the starting point. Its verified upload intent and existing-product target remain unchanged. Stage and verify one final PSD and matching JSON, then apply the selected-revision replacement and recoverable cleanup instructions emitted by `design_review_prompt.py`. Keep the original until verification succeeds.
